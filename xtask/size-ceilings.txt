# The size ceilings `cargo xtask size` enforces (docs/spec.md §6), in bytes
# of the shipped builds (`wasm-opt -Oz` on wasm).
#
# Each artifact's ceiling is the sum of its lines below the history. To move
# a ceiling, APPEND a line `<artifact> <+bytes or -bytes> <why>`, the why
# naming the change, its measurement and the owner decision, as the history
# does; never edit or remove a line. `.gitattributes` merges this file as a
# union, so when two branches each raise a ceiling, a rebase keeps both
# raises and their sum is the new ceiling, with no conflict.
#
# Artifacts: hello.wasm (wasm32-unknown-unknown `hello`, the primary
# budget), mm_wasm_guest.wasm and mm_wasm_guest_bundle.wasm (the guests on
# wasm32-unknown-unknown), hello (native `hello`, gated on
# x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu only). Until 2026-11-12 the ceilings were the
# constants WASM_BUDGET_BYTES, GUEST_BUDGET_BYTES, BUNDLE_GUEST_BUDGET_BYTES
# and SIZE_BUDGET_BYTES in xtask/src/main.rs; their records follow,
# unchanged, and the history names them.
